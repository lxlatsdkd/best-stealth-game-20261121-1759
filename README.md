The Quiet Gallery — a top-down stealth game set in a museum after dark. You are a thief with three galleries to rob: slip past patrolling guards and their lantern-lit vision cones, hide behind display cases, statues and plants, crouch through the dark corners where the detection meter fills slowly, steal the exhibit, then reach the green exit unseen. Three levels of rising difficulty (more guards, wider cones, less cover), a detection meter that gives you a chance to break line of sight and recover, an alarm chase when you are fully spotted, and tiny WebAudio sound effects — all hand-drawn on canvas, static files, offline, keyboard and mouse.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD or arrow keys, hold Shift to crouch, R to restart the level, P to pause, M to mute.
